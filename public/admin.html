Classification Thresholds Tuning

1. Reading Rate Baseline (words / sec)

Baseline adult reading speed used to compute expected dwell time (words / wps). 3.5 words/sec is ~210 WPM.

2. Minimum Dwell Time Fraction

If student spends less than this fraction of expected dwell time, section is flagged for potential skimming.

ratio (e.g. 0.40 = 40% of expected reading time)

3. Scroll Velocity Limits & Relative Ratio

Thresholds for flagging rapid scrolling past text sections.

4. Reread Revisit Dwell (REQ-3.3)

Minimum dwell time on second pass (revisit after reading forward) required to classify section as Reread.
