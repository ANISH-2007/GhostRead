How GhostRead Works

Welcome to GhostRead! Unlike ordinary PDF readers, GhostRead quietly verifies your reading absorption:

  • Dwell Time: Measures how long you spend on each section relative to its word count.
  • Scroll Speed: Calculates your scroll velocity relative to page text density.
  • Targeted Re-assessment: If you skim past a section, a brief micro-quiz will focus only on that section so you can reinforce your understanding.
💡 Teacher / Demo Tip: Toggle Demo Mode HUD in the top toolbar to see the live classification updates in real time as you scroll!
